# Brutus Tracker

Kill stats for Brutus, the cow boss in the Lumbridge cow field, and his Demonic hard mode.

A small overlay appears while you fight Brutus and shows, for each variant you have killed this session:

| Line | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Kills | Kills this session |
| KC (all time) | Your all-time kill count, read from the game's kill count message |
| Last | Time of your most recent kill |
| Best (session) | Your fastest kill this session |
| PB | Your fastest kill ever |
| Kills/hr | Kill rate since your first hit on Brutus this session |

Kill times run from your first hit on Brutus to his death.

## Settings

- **Show overlay** turns the overlay on or off.
- **Displayed stats** has one checkbox per line, so you can show only the stats you care about.

The overlay hides itself about a minute after Brutus was last seen. Hold Alt and drag it to move it.

## What is saved

Your personal best and all-time kill count are saved per RuneScape profile and survive restarts. Session stats (kills, last, session best, kills per hour) reset when the plugin or client restarts or you log into a different account.

## What it does not do

Brutus Tracker only reports finished kills. It gives no attack, special attack, positioning, or sound cues. For the respawn timer use the core **Boss Timers** plugin, and for drops use the core **Loot Tracker**.
